Hello! Today, I’m going to make a simple mini fan using a brushless motor. First, gather the motor, battery, wires, and a propeller. Second, connect the wires to the motor. Third, attach the propeller to the motor. Fourth, connect the wires to the battery. Finally, turn the fan on. It works! But remember, never touch the propeller while it’s spinning—it’s dangerous. Take note: if the propeller comes into contact with wood or paper, it will cut through them, so please be careful—just as you would be careful when looking after someone you care about. Thanks for watching this simple tutorial!
